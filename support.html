App language

The app follows your device language. Change it in Settings > App language. Speech-recognition languages are selected separately.

How to add the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  2. Add New Keyboard → Select Fordy Flow Keyboard
  3. Fordy Flow Keyboard → Enable Allow Full Access

Full Access shares temporary results with the app. Text surrounding the cursor in other apps is not collected.

Groq connection

Sends audio and transcripts to Groq.

Core features require iOS 18 or later; the voice keyboard requires iOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence features require a supported device, language and ready system model. Local models are downloaded separately. Groq requires your own account and API key, and its pricing and usage limits apply.

Recovery center

Use Recovery center to find interrupted recordings and retry from the original. Do not delete the app while resolving a recording issue.
