FORDY FLOW / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Operator: Kim BJ
Contact: ujbj84@gmail.com

1. Overview

Fordy Flow provides a voice keyboard, meeting and interview recording, transcription and meeting notes. Fordy operates no server or user-account service. Local mode processes recordings and text on your device. Groq processing is optional and is used when you connect your own API key and select that processing method.

2. Information stored on your device

Original recordings, transcripts, speaker names, meeting notes, folders, tags, vocabulary and app settings are kept in the app’s device storage. Voice-keyboard commands and temporary results are shared only between the app and its keyboard extension through their shared App Group. Your Groq API key is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain and is not included in the keyboard extension, ordinary settings files or diagnostic reports.

3. Optional Groq processing and international processing

When you choose Groq, audio is sent to the speech-recognition API at api.groq.com. Transcripts, speaker names, meeting input, and the user instructions, target-app information and vocabulary hints needed for text refinement are sent to the Groq API only as needed for the request. The API key authenticates your Groq account. Requests do not pass through a Fordy relay server.
Groq Inc. retains service-usage metadata. Groq states that inputs and outputs are not retained by default, but may be retained for up to 30 days for system reliability checks or abuse investigations, or as required by law. Retained customer data is stored in the United States. Actual retention depends on your Groq account and Data Controls settings. You manage Groq’s Zero Data Retention setting through Groq; Fordy does not enable it for you or guarantee zero retention for every account.
Data policy: https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data
Privacy policy: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
You can stop future Groq requests by switching to local mode and deleting the API key in Settings. Requests about information already sent to Groq are handled under that service’s policies.

4. Model downloads and Apple features

Local speech-recognition and speaker-identification models are downloaded from Apple’s model/asset distribution services or Hugging Face model repositories when you prepare them. Network-request information such as your IP address may be disclosed to those providers. Your recordings and transcripts are not uploaded to model repositories to download models. Apple speech recognition and Apple Intelligence depend on supported devices, OS versions, languages and system-model readiness.

5. Keyboard Full Access

The keyboard uses Full Access to share recording commands and transcription results with the Fordy app. Typed content is not used for advertising, tracking or data sales. Ordinary typing does not require a Groq API key or cloud processing. iOS may use the system keyboard in secure text fields and other restricted contexts.

6. Optional search and diagnostics

If you enable system search, recording titles, creation dates and durations are registered in Core Spotlight on your device. Audio and transcript text are not added to the search index.
Optional diagnostics store OS/app versions, recording-duration categories, engines, processing types, and success/failure/cancellation states on-device. Audio, transcript text, titles, speaker names, folder names, vocabulary words and API keys are excluded and are not automatically sent anywhere. If you choose to share a report, it is sent to the recipient you select. When you contact support by email, the email address, inquiry and attachments you provide are used to respond and troubleshoot.

7. Retention and deletion

Recordings remain until you delete them. Deleted recordings can be removed after your chosen Recently Deleted retention period (7, 30 or 90 days), or permanently deleted by you. Temporary voice-input data is cleaned up when processing finishes. For retries, data from ordinary failures may be kept for up to 10 minutes, and data waiting for a Groq rate limit may be kept until 10 minutes after the limit resets. Disabling optional diagnostics lets you delete those local records. Deleting a model does not delete original recordings.
iPhone/Watch transfers and backups may be affected by your device and Apple backup settings. Copies you export or save to other apps or cloud services must be managed separately at those locations.

8. Advertising, tracking and sale of information

The app uses no advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs and does not sell personal information. External processing, download and sharing services you choose are governed by their own policies.

9. Your choices and contact

You can change processing methods, delete recordings, models and API keys, and turn off search and diagnostics in the app. For access, correction, deletion or processing inquiries, contact ujbj84@gmail.com. Fordy cannot remotely access recordings or keys stored only on your device.

10. Changes

We update this policy and its effective date when features or processing methods change. Significant changes to external transfers are also disclosed in the app.
