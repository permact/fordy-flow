FORDY FLOW / iPHONE + APPLE WATCH

Voice Keyboard & Meeting Notes

Speak to type. Keep your conversations. A voice keyboard and meeting recorder that follow your language.

iOS 18+ · Voice Input iOS 26+
01

Voice keyboard

Dictate into other apps, or type with the built-in keyboard.

02

Recording

Record meetings and interviews, or import audio and video for transcription.

03

Edit recording

Identify speakers, then review and edit transcripts and notes.

Choose how to process. Local mode works on-device. Groq mode uses your own API key and sends the audio, transcripts and other information needed for processing directly to Groq. Original meeting recordings stay on your device.

Available in English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German and French. Follows your device language by default, with an in-app language setting. Choose speech-recognition languages separately.

Core features require iOS 18 or later; the voice keyboard requires iOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence features require a supported device, language and ready system model. Local models are downloaded separately. Groq requires your own account and API key, and its pricing and usage limits apply.

The first version is free. Obtain participant consent and follow your organization’s policies before recording.
